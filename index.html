Our Story

Two Brothers,
One Address.

Nestled in the heart of Bara Bazar, Baghpat, D.K. Photo Stat & Book Depot has been a cornerstone of the local community — two shops, side by side, run by brothers Neeraj Kumar Jain and Dheeraj Kumar Jain.

Together, they cover everything a student, job-seeker, or local business could need — from Xerox of any size and lamination, to all-brand stationery, spiral binding, and expert form filling for government exams and private job applications.
